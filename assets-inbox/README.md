# Assets inbox

Drop raw brand files here; they get optimised into `public/` before use.

- `abu-omar/` — Abu Omar renders (PNG/JPG, plain white background). The background is cut out and the
  result saved as `public/abu-omar/abu-omar.webp` (full figure), plus `avatar.webp` (head, 160px) and the
  `public/blog/abu-*.webp` cover crops. Keep `abuOmarImage` in `src/content/site.ts` in sync with its size.
- `brand/` — logo (SVG or high-resolution PNG preferred).
- `projects/`, `team/` — screenshots and photos.
