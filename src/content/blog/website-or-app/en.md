The first question most business owners ask is: **do I need a website or an app?** The answer depends on one thing: what will your customer do, and how often?

## When a website is enough

A website is your front door on the internet. Start with one if your goal is to:

- **Be found on Google** when people search for your service in Amman or across Jordan.
- **Show your services, work and prices** in a professional way that builds trust.
- **Let customers reach you fast** through WhatsApp, a call button or a request form.
- **Sell online** with an e-commerce store, without asking anyone to install anything.

A website works on every device, needs no app-store approval, and costs less to build and maintain.

## When you need a mobile app

An app makes sense when customers come back **regularly**, for example:

- Weekly or daily orders (restaurants, delivery, groceries).
- A service that needs **instant notifications**, like order status or payment reminders.
- Features that rely on the phone itself: **camera, biometrics, location**.

From our work: the [Shahed](/en/projects/shahed) installments app needs ID scanning, biometric sign-in and payment reminders, so an app was the right choice.

## And business systems?

If the problem is **inside your business** rather than reaching customers (stock, invoices, staff, appointments), you need a **system** that runs in the browser — like [Warshatak](/en/projects/warshatak), which runs a garage from car check-in to delivery.

## The short version

| If your goal is | Start with |
|---|---|
| Getting found and winning new customers | A website |
| Customers who return every week | A mobile app |
| Organizing your operations | A system (SaaS) |
