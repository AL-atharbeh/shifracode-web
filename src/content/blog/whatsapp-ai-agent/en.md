Every slow reply is an opportunity your competitor might take. And customers message at any hour: after midnight, on Fridays, and at peak time when everyone is busy.

## What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

It's an AI-powered "employee" that reads your customers' messages and replies instantly **using your store's knowledge**: your products, prices, opening hours, and delivery and return policies.

It works across channels: **WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram**, and even voice calls.

## What can it do?

- **Answer repeated questions** about prices, sizes and locations.
- **Search your catalog** and suggest the right product.
- **Calculate prices**, including live ones like today's gold rate.
- **Capture interested customers** so your team can follow up.
- **Understand voice notes** and reply in Arabic voice.

## Most importantly: when does it stop?

A good agent doesn't guess. If it doesn't have the information, it says so and **hands the chat to a human**. When a staff member steps in, the agent stays silent until the chat is handed back.

That's what separates a professional agent from ordinary bots that confidently give wrong answers.

## Who is it for?

Restaurants, gold shops, clothing stores, clinics — any business that gets lots of messages every day.
